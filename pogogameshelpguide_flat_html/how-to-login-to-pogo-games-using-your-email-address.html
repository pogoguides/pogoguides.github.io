How to Login to Pogo Games Using Your Email Address?

Welcome to this Pogo Playbook guide. This independent guide provides general information to help you understand the account access process and common questions about Pogo Games.

Before You Begin

Make sure you have access to the email address associated with your account and are using the correct sign-in details. Always use the official Pogo or EA account sign-in experience when entering account credentials.

How to Get Started

  1. Open the official Pogo Games website in an up-to-date browser.
  2. Select the sign-in option and enter the email address or username associated with your account, as applicable.
  3. Enter your password carefully and check for typing errors, including capitalization.
  4. Complete any verification step displayed on the sign-in screen.
  5. After signing in, open the game or account section you want to use.

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Check that your internet connection is stable.
  • Refresh the page and try again.
  • Update your browser and enable the site features required for the game.
  • If you have forgotten your password, use the official account recovery process.
  • If the issue continues, consult the official Pogo or EA help resources.
Account safety: Never share your password or verification codes. This is an independent guide and is not an official Pogo or EA support page.
